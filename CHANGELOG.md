## 0.1.1 — 2026-09-04

Compresso's background helper is now optional.

### New

- **Keep agents connected after quit**, a new switch in Settings. It is on by
  default, and a small helper keeps Claude Code and Codex working while
  Compresso is closed. Turn it off and quitting Compresso restores your agents'
  original settings. Opening Compresso reconnects them.

### Improved

- Compresso repairs your agents' settings at startup if another program has
  taken its local port.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-03

The first version of **Compresso**.

### New

- Local optimizer for **Claude Code** and **Codex**: requests route through a
  loopback port on your Mac, redundant context is compressed away, and the
  request continues to Anthropic or OpenAI under your own credentials.
- Menu-bar savings: tokens removed and estimated cost impact, per agent, per day.
- Honest numbers: measured token counts and estimated ≈$ are reported
  separately, never blended.
- Fail-open by design: if the optimizer is down, starting, or your subscription
  lapses, requests pass straight through. Never a dead port.
- Clean uninstall: restores every config it changed and lists everything it
  will remove before it runs.
- 5-day free trial, no card. Billing by Polar as merchant of record.
